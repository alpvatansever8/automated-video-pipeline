# Iceland

Iceland is an island nation in the North Atlantic, sitting right on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge where the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates slowly drift apart.

Volcanoes, glaciers, black sand beaches and geothermal springs shape the landscape, which is why the country is often called the land of fire and ice.

# Reykjavik

Reykjavik is the capital and largest city of Iceland, and it is the northernmost capital of a sovereign state in the world.

The colorful rooftops of the old town and the tall tower of Hallgrimskirkja church are among the most recognizable views of the city.

# Thingvellir National Park

Thingvellir National Park lies in a rift valley where the continental plates meet, and visitors can walk between two continents along the cracks in the rock.

It is also the site where Iceland's parliament, the Althing, first gathered in the year 930.

# Vatnajokull Glacier

Vatnajokull is the largest ice cap in Europe by volume, covering about eight percent of the country.

Beneath its ice lie active volcanoes, and at its edge glacial lagoons fill with floating icebergs that wash up as bright blue ice on the nearby black sand beach.
